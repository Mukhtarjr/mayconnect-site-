Your Privacy Matters

How Your Data Is Handled

We aim to handle account-deletion requests carefully while protecting users and complying with applicable obligations.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the platform, the account and the applicable obligations, an approved request may cover:

  • Account profile information and associated login identifiers.
  • Personal information that is no longer needed for a lawful purpose.
  • Eligible saved preferences and associated account records.
  • Other eligible personal data associated with the account.
The precise data categories depend on each platform's systems and records. Account deletion must be carried out in the relevant platform's backend and connected systems; removing an app from a phone does not delete the associated account.

Information That May Need to Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained where there is a valid legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reason.

  • Financial and transaction records that must be retained to meet applicable legal or accounting requirements.
  • Records necessary to resolve an outstanding transaction, dispute or suspected fraudulent activity.
  • Information subject to a valid legal obligation or other applicable retention requirement.

Retained information should be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the applicable retention period. The relevant platform's actual retention periods must be established and followed by the company.

Requesting Partial Data Deletion

If you want eligible personal data deleted but wish to keep your account, select that option in the form. Explain the data you want deleted. Some records may not be eligible for deletion if they must be retained for a valid legal or operational reason.
